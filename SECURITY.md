# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

We provide Long-Term Support (LTS) for the Qbee agent on a 2-year rolling basis. Releases follow the `YYYY.WW` versioning format (where `YYYY` represents the release year and `WW` represents the calendar week). 

Any version newer than two years receives backwards-compatible updates and security patches. Versions older than two years are considered End-of-Life (EOL) and receive no further updates.

| Version | Supported |
| ------- | --------- |
| `YYYY.WW` (Released within past 2 years) | :white_check_mark: |
| `YYYY.WW` (Older than 2 years) | :x: |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of Qbee agent seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure.

* **Reporting Guidelines:** To report a potential security issue, please review and follow our official [Security Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](https://docs.qbee.io/security-vulnerability-disclosure-policy.html).
* **What to Expect:**
  * **Acknowledgement:** Our team will review your submission and acknowledge receipt promptly.
  * **Assessment & Updates:** We will investigate the report, evaluate its impact, and keep you updated as we work toward a solution.
  * **Remediation:** If verified, we will issue backwards-compatible update to the agent with a fix.
* **Coordinated Disclosure:** We ask that you refrain from sharing details publicly until our security team has evaluated and addressed the reported issue.
